Status: curated interim image set approved by owner, 11 Aug 2026.

## Usage policy

- Interim customer-catalogue imagery only; replace with Shiloh-owned photography when available.
- Source provider: Pexels unless explicitly noted otherwise.
- Pexels states its uploaded photos/videos are licensed under the Pexels licence and may be used for personal and commercial purposes without attribution.
- Do not imply that a depicted person is a Shiloh client/practitioner or endorses Shiloh.
- Avoid visible third-party branding, before/after claims, or misleading treatment-result representations.
- For sensitive/intimate services, prefer neutral clinic/equipment/wellness imagery rather than identifiable treatment subjects.
- Store served copies under Shiloh control rather than depending on third-party hotlinks.

## Sensitive-service rule

For Areola Reconstruction, Pelvic Floor Strengthening and intimate HIFU, do not use identifiable treatment-in-progress stock photography. Use a neutral consultation/clinic/equipment image or no image until a Shiloh-approved purpose-specific asset is available.

## Deployment rule

A source page being listed here does not by itself authorize a database `image_url` change. Before production use, obtain the actual image asset from the source, retain a Shiloh-controlled served copy, verify the served asset visually, then map the resulting Shiloh URL to CRM services. This prevents fragile hotlinks and keeps the provenance record tied to the exact chosen source.
